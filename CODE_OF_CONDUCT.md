# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project exists to make public systems legible. Everyone contributing to
it — regardless of background, identity, or experience — deserves a
harassment-free experience.

## Expected behavior

- Engage with the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith in disagreement, and disagree with evidence.
- Respect the scope boundary. It protects people, including people you may
  disagree with.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or targeted intimidation.
- Publishing private information about any individual, in issues, PRs,
  commits, or data. This is both a conduct violation and a scope violation.
- Using this project or its data to surveil, locate, or target individuals.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through a private channel listed in the README.
Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences range from a warning to a
permanent ban depending on severity.
